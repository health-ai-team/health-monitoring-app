# AI Health Monitoring & Patient-Doctor Connection Platform
# Quick Start Guide

## 1. Purpose

This is the entry point for all team members and Freebuff AI coding agents.

The project is an AI-powered health-monitoring application that connects patients and doctors through a Gemma 4-powered AI assistant.

The application allows:

- Patients to record daily health information.
- Patients to provide health information through surveys, structured input, or natural-language AI conversations.
- Gemma 4 to use relevant historical context to provide personalized, non-diagnostic guidance.
- Doctors to view authorized patient information through charts, trends, summaries, and alerts.
- Deterministic backend safety rules to detect predefined dangerous thresholds.
- Authorized doctors and, where configured and permitted, emergency contacts to receive alerts.
- Patient health data, survey responses, and AI conversations to be stored in a central database.
- Relevant authorized information to be retrieved from that database for AI context.

## 5. Golden Rule

Do not ask Freebuff to "build the whole app" from one prompt.

Instead:

1. Inspect.
2. Understand.
3. Plan.
4. Review the plan.
5. Implement one bounded task.
6. Test.

8. Commit.
9. Integrate.

## 6. Ownership Rule

Each workstream primarily modifies its own files.

If a task requires changes to another workstream:

1. Stop.
2. Identify the required change.
3. Document the integration requirement.
4. Coordinate with the owner.
5. Agree on the interface or contract.
6. Make the smallest required change.
7. Test the integration.

Never silently modify another workstream's architecture.

## 7. Critical Safety Rule

Gemma 4 is an AI assistant.

Gemma 4:

- Does not diagnose.
- Does not directly write to the database.
- Does not independently decide that a medical safety threshold has been crossed.
- Does not independently trigger critical emergency alerts.
- Does not replace professional medical judgment.

Critical safety alerts are generated by deterministic backend rules based on explicitly approved thresholds.

Freebuff must never invent medical safety thresholds.
